Finance for iPhone

How can we help?

Finance works entirely on your device. These answers cover the most common questions about records, backups, imports, and privacy.

Email support

Where is my data?

Your records are stored on your iPhone. Finance has no account or server to sign in to.

How do I make a backup?

Open More → Settings → Data & export, then create a JSON backup and save it somewhere you control.

How do I import a statement?

Open Data & export and choose a CSV or PDF statement. Review every suggested row before saving it.

How do annual bills work?

Add the payment as an annual recurring item. A sinking-fund goal can show the amount to set aside each month.

Can I try fictional data?

Yes. Open More → Settings → Explore with sample data. It stays separate from your own records.

How do I delete everything?

Open More → Settings → Data & export and use the delete option. Removing the app also removes its local data.

Contact

For help, bug reports, or privacy questions, email zyh_michelle@126.com. Include the app version shown at the bottom of Settings, but do not send financial records unless you intentionally choose to.
