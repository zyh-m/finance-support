Privacy Policy

Finance is a local-first personal finance app. The developer does not collect, transmit, sell, rent, or share information from the app.

Information stored on your device

Information you enter—including accounts, transactions, income, debts, goals, categories, exchange rates, shared expenses, settings, and attachments—is stored locally on your device. It is used only to provide the features you request inside the app.

Photos and documents

Finance accesses a receipt photo or statement file only after you choose it. Receipt recognition and statement reading occur on the device. Imported statement files are read for the import you requested and are not retained. Attachments you explicitly choose to keep remain in the app’s local storage.

Notifications and device security

Alerts are scheduled locally using Apple’s notification system. Notification previews may be visible on your Lock Screen according to your device settings.

If you enable app locking, Face ID, Touch ID, or your device passcode is evaluated by Apple’s LocalAuthentication framework. Finance does not receive or store biometric data.

Backups and exports

An iCloud or computer backup of your device may include Finance data under Apple’s backup settings and policies. Files you explicitly export or back up are controlled by the destination you choose after they leave the app.

Retention and deletion

Records remain on your device until you delete them through the app’s Data & export screen or remove the app. You can revoke camera, photo, Face ID, and notification permissions through iOS Settings.

Third parties and tracking

Finance contains no advertising, third-party analytics, tracking technology, bank connection, or developer-operated account service. The app does not send your information to third parties.

Children

Finance is a general-purpose budgeting tool and is not directed to children. Because the developer does not collect information from the app, the developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the effective date and policy on this page will be updated.
